# Sales, channel and commercial analysis review

Identify the decision the artifact recommends, then test what could change that decision.

## Audit checks

- Every material conclusion states its assumptions, data source, sample size, time range, market scope, and metric definition.
- Pipeline records distinguish stage, probability, owner, next action, timing, and actual evidence of conversion.
- Forecast distinguishes baseline, target, and upside; interest, pipeline, shipment, sell-in, sell-through, revenue, GMV, and cash collection are not conflated.
- Price, discount, cost, freight, platform fee, tax, rebate, exchange rate, returns, warranty cost, and payment terms use a consistent basis.
- Unit economics can be independently recalculated and includes the decision-relevant costs.
- Sensitivity analysis identifies variables capable of changing the recommendation.
- A small number of customers, channels, months, campaigns, or public examples is not extrapolated without qualification.
- Competitor pricing and channel claims are current, market-specific, and sourced.
- Confidence reflects the strength of evidence, not writing certainty.
- Decision-critical missing information is explicit: inventory, channel capability, demand validation, refund rate, support burden, margin, working capital, competitive response, or operational constraint.

## Decision reversal table

Use when it makes the result clearer:

| Current assumption or claim | Evidence used | What happens if false? | Minimum verification | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Focus first on inputs that are both uncertain and capable of reversing “enter/do not enter”, “invest/do not invest”, “launch/wait”, or “choose A/B”.

Do not convert an unknown pipeline probability, market size, conversion rate, price, cost, or margin into a confident recommendation.

## Human judgment boundary

People decide strategic priority, resource ceiling, acceptable loss, relationship value, negotiation position, and whether the evidence threshold is sufficient to place the bet.
